Who We Are

Finally Free Fellowship

Finally Free Fellowship is a Christ-centered, men’s recovery program for sexual addiction and pornography. We are a free, peer-led group built on the SA 12-step model and the Conquerors curriculum. We meet seven days a week on Zoom, with brothers in multiple countries. We are not a replacement for your church — we are a tool your men can use to get free.

See Our Meetings Contact Us
📈
The Scale of the Problem
Studies show 50–68% of men in the average congregation struggle with pornography. Most will never raise their hand in church. They are silently losing the battle alone.
🙏
Why Men Don’t Ask for Help
Shame. Fear of judgment. Not knowing where to turn. Finally Free provides a safe, anonymous, judgment-free space where men can be honest about their struggle and find brothers who understand.
⚔
What We Offer Your Men
Free weekly Zoom meetings, a structured recovery curriculum, daily accountability, a global brotherhood, and a path to real freedom — all grounded in Scripture and the 12 Steps.
🌱
What It Costs Your Church
Nothing. Finally Free is entirely self-supporting through the voluntary contributions of its members. No fees, no licensing, no curriculum purchases required. Just point your men to us.
👥
How It Complements Your Ministry
We are not a church or a counseling service. We don’t compete with your ministry — we serve it. Our men become better husbands, fathers, and congregants as they find freedom. Many reconnect with their faith through the program.
🌐
A Global Brotherhood
Brothers from across the US, Singapore, and beyond meet every night of the week. Your men can attend any meeting, any day, without leaving home. No waiting list. No intake form. Just show up.
How to Partner With Us
1
Attend a Meeting Yourself
See what your men will experience. Attend any Finally Free meeting this week as a guest. No commitment required. Just come and observe the brotherhood.
2
Share With Your Men
Mention Finally Free from the pulpit, in men’s ministry, or one-on-one with men you know are struggling. Share this app link: sstolle24.github.io/FFfellowship
3
Start an FF Group at Your Church
We can help you launch a local Finally Free group that meets in person or on Zoom. We provide the curriculum, training, and ongoing support. Your church provides the space and the men.
4
Connect With Our Leadership
Reach out to discuss how Finally Free can serve your congregation specifically. We would love to hear your vision and share how we can partner together for lasting freedom.
